How to play Checkers

This is a two-player checkers game played by taking turns on the same screen. It uses American checkers rules, except each player starts with 8 pieces instead of 12.

The board and starting position

The board is 8×8 squares. Pieces only ever sit on the dark squares. White starts on the two rows nearest the top, and Black starts on the two rows nearest the bottom. The middle four rows start empty. Black always moves first.

Moving a piece

  • Click one of your pieces to select it. Its legal squares light up green.
  • Click a lit-up square to move there.
  • Clicking anywhere else does nothing.
  • Clicking a different piece of yours switches your selection.
  • A normal piece moves one square diagonally forward only — never backward or sideways.

Jumping (capturing)

If an enemy piece sits diagonally next to yours, and the square right behind it is empty, you can jump over it to capture it.

  • Jumping is mandatory. If any of your pieces can jump, you must jump — a quiet move isn't allowed. Pieces that can jump are outlined in red.
  • If more than one jump is available, you choose which one to take.
  • Multi-jump: if the piece you just jumped with can jump again immediately, it must keep jumping in the same turn. No other piece may move until the chain ends.

Kings

A piece that reaches the far row becomes a king, shown with a crown. A king can move and jump one square diagonally in any of the four directions, not just forward.

If a piece is crowned in the middle of a multi-jump, its turn ends right away, even if the new king could technically keep jumping.

How the game ends

  • Win: if the player to move has no pieces left, or has pieces but no legal move (including no legal jump), the other player wins.
  • Draw: if 40 turns in a row pass with no capture and no piece is newly crowned, the game is a draw.

Use the New game button at any time to reset the board and start over.
