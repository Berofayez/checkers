# #6: Kings

Status: done

## Goal
A piece that reaches the far row becomes a king, and kings can move and jump
in any diagonal direction.

## Done when
- [x] A Black piece reaching row 0, or a White piece reaching row 7, becomes
      a king and is shown with a crown mark.
- [x] A king can move one square diagonally in any of the four directions
      (forward or backward).
- [x] A king can jump in any of the four diagonal directions, following the
      same capture and mandatory-jump rules as normal pieces.
- [x] If a piece is promoted to king in the middle of a multi-jump, its turn
      ends immediately even if a further jump would otherwise be available.
